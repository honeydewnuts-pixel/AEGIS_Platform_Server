# Data integrity rules (research)

1. Preserve original datasets and ledgers; never silently overwrite results.
2. Verify SHA-256 against `research/checkpoint/SHA256SUMS.txt` after import or transfer.
3. Distinguish exploratory, reconstructed, reproduced, validated, approved, integrated, and server-verified work.
4. Large Bid/Ask and cash ledgers are evidence — do not replace with summaries alone.
5. Do not commit secrets, API keys, or private customer credentials into research/.
